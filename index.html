Science of Memory

FSRS Spaced Repetition & Active Recall

تكرار متباعد ذكي واستدعاء نشط لترسيخ المفردات مدى الحياة

Never forget a word again. Lingoa applies the state-of-the-art Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS) to calculate your personalized forgetting curve and schedules flashcard reviews right when your brain needs them.

  • FSRS Memory Grading: Again (<1m), Hard (<5m), Good (<10m), Easy (15d) intervals tailored to your retention rate.
  • Type Recall Practice: Reinforce spelling with hands-on typing and instant feedback.
  • Custom Decks: Create your own tailored study decks with one tap.
Authentic Context

Native Speaker Videos & Real Collocations

شاهد متحدثين أصليين واستوعب التراكيب الشائعة والأفعال الاصطلاحية

Words don't live in isolation. Watch short native speaker clips from real-world talks for every vocabulary entry. Discover common collocations, phrasal verbs, and idioms with native audio and Arabic translations.

  • TED Context Clips: Multiple authentic clips with synchronized subtitles for each target word.
  • Collocations & Idioms: Learn natural phrasing like "add salt", "add to the list", and "add insult to injury".
  • Pronunciation & Speaking: Listen to crystal-clear audio and practice speaking with instant voice checks.
