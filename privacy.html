2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

Lingoa is built on a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture. You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details to learn vocabulary with Lingoa.

A. On-Device Learning Data (Stored Locally Only)

  • Data Collected: Your study progress, mastered words, daily streak counts, quiz scores, spaced repetition (SRS) schedules, and personal bookmarks.
  • Storage: Stored 100% locally on your device in a secure SQLite database. This data is never transmitted to our servers and remains completely in your control.

B. Audio and Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Purpose: Used strictly for interactive pronunciation practice, speech recognition, and vocabulary speaking exercises.
  • Privacy Protection: Audio is analyzed in real-time on your device (or via native system speech recognition). We never record, save, store, or transmit your voice or audio recordings to any external server.

C. Local Notifications and Alarms

  • Purpose: Used locally on your device to schedule daily study reminders and spaced repetition reviews.
  • Privacy Protection: Operates entirely offline on your phone without external network transmission.

D. Diagnostic & Crash Telemetry (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • Data Collected: Anonymous device state at the time of an unexpected crash, operating system version, device model, and error stack traces.
  • Purpose: To ensure high performance, resolve technical bugs, and maintain app stability.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep Lingoa’s comprehensive learning library accessible to all learners worldwide for free, we display in-app advertisements via Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC).

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by AdMob to deliver relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and cap ad repetition.
  • Device Telemetry: Device model, screen dimensions, operating system, and coarse location (country/city level derived from IP).

How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via your Android device settings: Settings ➔ Google (or Privacy) ➔ Ads ➔ Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy  |  How Google uses information from sites or apps.

4. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play)

Lingoa offers an optional Pro Subscription which removes all ads and unlocks unlimited access to all features.

  • Secure Google Play Billing: All financial transactions are processed securely through Google Play. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment card details.
  • Receipt Verification via RevenueCat: We partner with RevenueCat, Inc. to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat processes anonymous tokens and purchase receipts to unlock Pro privileges across your sessions. Review the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
